Stroke Brain Atlas Live · Oct 2026

C-STROKE

The world's first 3D cellular-level atlas of the stroke brain

  • Web platform for the cellular-level 3D stroke brain atlas built at the Sudha Gopalakrishnan Brain Centre, IIT Madras.
  • Brings scientific data and interactive visualisation together so researchers can explore brain images and anatomical structures.
  • Helps researchers study how stroke changes the human brain.
  • Launched by the Hon'ble Prime Minister of India, Shri Narendra Modi, at IIT Madras on 11 October 2026. Co-author of the research preprint.

Human Brainstem Atlas Live · 2026

ANCHOR

Atlas of Neurochemical Characterization of the Human Brainstem with 3D Reconstruction

  • Interactive platform for exploring multimodal brainstem datasets through one unified interface.
  • Visualisation and navigation of high-resolution MRI, block-face imaging, histology and immunohistochemistry.
  • Tools for anatomical annotations, neurochemical maps and synchronised multimodal views.
  • Launched at the III BRICS Neuroscience Symposium 2026. Co-author of the scientific publication.

Hospital Management & Appointment Booking System

A full-stack web app that moves a clinic's bookings, records and front desk online
